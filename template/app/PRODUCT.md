# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MotionPress is built for content operations teams that produce AI-assisted motion assets and publish search-ready articles. The primary roles are administrators who configure integrations and govern usage, editors who manage the publishing workflow, and creators who generate animations and prepare content.

Their shared job is to move from an idea to a published, measurable asset without switching between unrelated demo tools or guessing whether a background task succeeded.

## Product Purpose

MotionPress is an AI animation and SEO publishing workspace. It combines prompt optimization, safe HTML animation generation, video rendering, asset management, article authoring, SEO quality checks, publishing automation, and operational monitoring in one coherent workflow.

Success means a new installation can expose missing configuration clearly, an authorized user can complete the full create-to-publish flow, every long-running action has an observable status and retry path, and administrators can understand usage, cost, failures, and permissions from the dashboard.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clear, efficient.

The product should feel calm under operational pressure, precise about system state, and confident without being theatrical. Copy should name the task and the consequence rather than advertise the interface.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS starter-kit pages that expose template features instead of a product workflow.
- Decorative AI-tool dashboards with gradients, glowing surfaces, oversized metrics, or ornamental animation.
- Demo controls, fake data, dead buttons, and settings forms that do not persist.
- Card-heavy layouts that fragment one workflow into unrelated boxes.
- Technical infrastructure labels such as AWS S3 or PgBoss in user-facing navigation.

## Design Principles

1. **One workflow, one shell.** Animation, publishing, tasks, and administration use one consistent workspace and navigation model.
2. **State is a product feature.** Configuration, queue, build, quota, and publication status must be visible, specific, and recoverable.
3. **Progressive complexity.** Creators see the next useful action; integration and governance controls stay with administrators.
4. **Real data only.** Empty states explain how to create data. The product never substitutes hard-coded events or template metrics.
5. **Quality before publish.** SEO, safety, permissions, and quota checks are part of the workflow, not post-release cleanup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, descriptive control labels, 44px mobile touch targets, sufficient color contrast, non-color status cues, localized English and Simplified Chinese copy, and reduced-motion preferences.
